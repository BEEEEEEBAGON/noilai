\paragraph{Models.}
The panel of \placeholder{17} self-hosted and \placeholder{4} API-served models (Table~\ref{tab:models}) maximizes the number of distinct tokenizers while pairing general multilingual models with Southeast Asian and Vietnamese-specialized ones of matched size, among them an interpretability ladder of Gemma~3 models covered by Gemma Scope~2 \citep{gemma-team-2025-gemma3,deepmind-2025-gemma-scope-2} and PhoGPT-4B-Chat with its 20,480-token vocabulary \citep{2311.02945}. A fine-tune whose tokenizer is byte-identical to its base's holds tokenization fixed while varying training data; with $n = 1$ such a pair suggests, never isolates. The panel is frozen after every family passes a smoke test; between-tokenizer claims need at least eight open models over four tokenizer families, one of them pass-through and not Gemma, and under a compute overrun models are cut in a pre-registered order and named (Appendix~\ref{app:compute}).

\paragraph{Prompting.}
Prompts are in Vietnamese and define the syllable structure, the variant step by step and the spelling rules, followed by three fixed engine-built demonstrations from base pairs that occur in no item (Appendix~\ref{app:prompts}). Three paraphrases carry the same content in different wording and order \citep{2310.11324}; results are the mean over paraphrases with the range, and a claim about a model holds only if its direction holds under all three. Ablations are zero-shot, name-only (the variant's name without its steps, separating cultural recall from rule-following), English instructions and character-spaced input; the explicit onset--rime--tone decomposition of each syllable is a main condition, the only one that separates rule knowledge from sub-token access. Decoding is greedy, and an output without an extractable answer counts as wrong, never as missing.

\paragraph{Re-encoding arms.}
The baseline is NFC text in the majority (older) placement convention. C1 rewrites it to NFD and C1$'$ to the partially composed form (fewer extra tokens than NFD, so the three are ordered in token count); C2 switches to the newer placement convention, which changes exactly the 69 syllable types of Appendix~\ref{app:rules} and is estimated on affected items; the contrast arms C3a and C3b strip the tone marks, or every diacritic, destroying information. The meaning-preserving arms are applied to the whole prompt, so that the item is not the one oddly encoded span in an otherwise normal message, which would itself be a cue; applying them to the item text alone is a secondary condition on a 500-item subset of one model, and the strip arms are item-only. Before any GPU time a \emph{census} classifies every panel tokenizer, on every engine path it runs through and a fixed 1,000-string probe set, as \emph{passing through} (different ids that decode back to the input), \emph{normalizing} (identical ids; the C1 effect is zero by construction and asserted, not tested) or \emph{corrupting} (decoding does not return the input; the arm is refused on that engine); the verdict is a first-class column of Table~\ref{tab:arms} (Appendix~\ref{app:methods}).

\paragraph{Scoring.}
\task{T1} compares parsed structures after canonicalizing Unicode form, tone-mark placement, case and the \vi{lí/lý} alternation, without repairing misspellings or equating regional homophones, and files every output under one of eleven error classes (Appendix~\ref{app:methods}). \task{T2} scores membership in the gold set and the variant class. \task{T3} scores the generated yes/no answer for every model, as accuracy and balanced accuracy; open models add two log-probability quantities, reported separately and never compared with the generated answer: a forced choice from the answer-token probabilities (\texttt{t3\_yesno\_lp}) and a BLiMP-style paired score over the two candidates' string log-probabilities under the shared \task{T1} prompt (\texttt{t3\_pair\_lp}), a likelihood contrast rather than a judgment \citep{2023-acl-long-230,2023-emnlp-main-306} that favours the shorter or more frequent twin and is therefore reported raw and length-normalized per token. No metric uses an LLM judge \citep{2025-findings-emnlp-587}; baselines are copy, reversal, a wrong-variant rule, a random legal output, the majority \task{T3} answer and the mean human.

\paragraph{Tokenizer profile.}
For every tokenizer we measure, over the standard syllable inventory in running-text position, tokens per syllable, the share of single-token syllables and, among split syllables, \emph{boundary alignment}: the share of internal token boundaries on an onset$|$rime, glide$|$nucleus or nucleus$|$coda seam, adapting the alignment metric of \citet{2026-acl-long-634} to the seams inside a syllable; a boundary between a base letter and its combining mark is never aligned, and under NFD we record whether the tone mark is a token of its own. Gemma~3 gives 1.78 tokens per syllable, 29.5\% single-token, alignment 0.92 among split syllables (0.94 over all syllables, a single-token syllable counting as 1.0) and an onset$|$rime split in 51.5\% of syllables under NFC, against 2.83, 7.1\%, 0.21 (0.27) and a tone mark isolated in 41.6\% under NFD; its identity normalizer leaves NFD ids identical to NFC ids for 1.3\% of the census strings and longer for 91\%, and every string decodes back to itself, so it passes through (Table~\ref{tab:tokaudit}). \placeholder{Every panel tokenizer after the freeze.}

\paragraph{Statistics and pre-registration.}
The base pair is the sampled unit and the cluster; paraphrases are aggregated to the item level. Every interval is a bootstrap over base pairs (2,000 replicates, BCa where a cell has at least 50 pairs) \citep{2411.00640}, every comparison is paired by item inside each replicate, and the primary inference for every paired contrast is that bootstrap or a paired $t$ on base-pair means, McNemar's mid-$p$ test \citep{P18-1128} being a secondary item-level statistic labelled as ignoring clustering. Holm's correction \citep{holm-1979-simple,Q17-1033} is applied within declared families: per model, the re-encoding cells of Table~\ref{tab:arms}, fourteen cells minus those undefined for the model; for \hyp{4} and \hyp{6}, the per-model tests across the panel; Table~\ref{tab:main} is estimation only. A claim about models in general rests on a hierarchical pooled estimate with models nested in tokenizer families (REML with the Hartung--Knapp adjustment, or a family-level bootstrap), reported with the number of families, never on a count of significant cells. \hyp{1} is a logistic regression of item correctness on whether the syllable is split, tokens per syllable centred within model and alignment among split syllables, with lexicality, corpus log frequency, variant and task, model fixed effects and model $\times$ fragmentation interactions, intervals from the base-pair cluster bootstrap, decided by a nested likelihood-ratio test and reported with and without the frequency term, which is nearly collinear with whole-token status. For the arms the headline is the within-item average treatment effect per model (Section~\ref{sec:counterfactuals}). At 70\% accuracy 350 items per cell resolve a 10-point contrast at 80\% power and the 125-item API cells 11--17 points, so cell-level claims rest on the open models; a 200-item development pilot fixes the intraclass correlation and discordance behind these sizes (\placeholder{pilot ICC and discordance}; Appendix~\ref{app:methods}). Generator, data, scoring and census are fixed in the commit that freezes the release, and hypotheses, analysis plan and probing protocol in a dated commit before any test-split run; later changes are logged deviations. Model outputs are never excluded, an item is removed only on a validator-confirmed generator bug with regeneration before any model run, a model is dropped only for a documented engineering failure and named, and sample sizes are fixed in advance, nothing being added, removed or repeated after a result has been seen (Appendix~\ref{app:prereg}).
