\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Models.}
The panel of \placeholder{17} self-hosted and \placeholder{4} API-served models is chosen to maximize the number of distinct tokenizers while pairing general multilingual models with Southeast Asian and Vietnamese-specialized models of matched size. Table~\ref{tab:models} lists the groups: an interpretability ladder of Gemma~3 instruction-tuned models (1B, 4B, 12B), whose every layer is covered by Gemma Scope~2 \citep{gemma-team-2025-gemma3,lieberum-etal-2024-gemma-scope,deepmind-2025-gemma-scope-2}; scaling series within two open families (Qwen~3.5 at four sizes, Gemma~4 at three); Southeast Asian fine-tunes (SEA-LION v4.5 E2B, Sailor2-8B); Vietnamese-first models (PhoGPT-4B-Chat with its 20,480-token vocabulary \citep{2311.02945}, Vistral-7B-Chat); a legacy baseline without official Vietnamese support (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct); two 27B open references served on a TPU; and four hosted models (two Gemini Flash variants, gpt-oss-120b and gpt-oss-20b) that return generations but no log-probabilities. A fine-tune whose tokenizer file is byte-identical to its base's (SEA-LION E2B vs.\ Gemma~4 E2B) holds tokenization fixed while varying training data; with $n = 1$ such a pair suggests, never isolates. The panel was frozen at Gate~1 with every checkpoint identifier and revision resolved; exact identifiers, serving engine, precision and quantization are recorded per run in a manifest and listed in Appendix~\ref{app:compute}. Gemma~3 is never run in fp16, whose activations overflow; no model receives a system prompt; chat templates are rendered in the runner so that the hashed string is exactly what every backend scores; thinking modes are detected from the template and off in the main runs; and about 200 items per quantized model are re-run in bf16 to measure drift.

\paragraph{Prompting.}
Prompts are written in Vietnamese and define the syllable structure, the variant step by step and the spelling rules, followed by three fixed demonstrations built by the engine from three base pairs that occur in no item (Appendix~\ref{app:prompts}). Three paraphrases of every template carry the same content in different wording and order, since formatting alone has moved accuracies by tens of points \citep{2310.11324}; results are reported as the mean over paraphrases with the range. Ablations use zero-shot, name-only (the variant's name without its steps, separating cultural recall from rule-following) and English-instruction versions, and a character-spaced input format. A further input format, the explicit onset--rime--tone decomposition of each syllable, is a main condition rather than an ablation: it is the only one that separates rule knowledge from sub-token access. Decoding is greedy with a 64-token cap. A regex extracts the last line beginning \vi{đáp án:} (`answer:'), after any thinking block is removed and counted; an output without an extractable answer counts as wrong, never as missing.

\paragraph{Re-encoding arms.}
The baseline is NFC text in the majority (older) placement convention. C1 rewrites the item text from NFC to NFD; C1$'$ rewrites it into the partially composed Windows-1258 form (\texttt{pc}), which adds fewer tokens than NFD, so that the three arms are ordered in token count; C2 rewrites it into the newer placement convention, which changes exactly the 69 syllable types of Appendix~\ref{app:rules} and nothing else and is estimated on affected items; the contrast arms C3a and C3b strip the tone marks, or every diacritic, from the item text, destroying information. Arms apply to the item text by default (rewriting the instruction as well is a secondary condition), and a run whose two arms produce byte-identical prompts is skipped and recorded as unchanged. Before any GPU time, a \emph{census} classifies every panel tokenizer on every engine path it runs through as \emph{passing through} (NFC and NFD receive different ids and decode back to the input), \emph{normalizing} (identical ids; the C1 effect is then zero by construction and is asserted, not tested) or \emph{corrupting} (decoding does not return the input; the arm is refused on that engine, since it would turn C1 into C3); native and library tokenizations of the same model are asserted equal. The census column of Table~\ref{tab:arms} is a first-class result.

\paragraph{Scoring and error taxonomy.}
\task{T1} compares parsed structures after a canonicalization that normalizes Unicode form, tone-mark placement, case and the \vi{lí/lý} alternation but deliberately does not repair misspellings and does not equate regional homophones. Every output receives one error class in a fixed precedence: unparseable, correct, lenient-only (right syllables, wrong order), copy of the input, plain reversal, the output of another of the six variants, a spelling violation on the right structure, a regional homophone spelling (\vi{d/gi}, \vi{ch/tr}, \vi{s/x}), an \vi{ay/ây} doublet, a component error naming the wrong onset, rime or tone, or an illegal syllable; per-component accuracy comes from the same parse at no annotation cost. \task{T2} scores membership in the gold set and the unordered variant class. \task{T3} scores the generated yes/no answer for every model, as accuracy and balanced accuracy; for open models we add forced-choice accuracy from the answer-token probabilities and BLiMP-style paired accuracy from the string log-probabilities of the two candidates \citep{2023-acl-long-230,2023-emnlp-main-306}, never compared across scoring modes. No metric uses an LLM judge, whose agreement across languages is poor \citep{2025-findings-emnlp-587}. Baselines are copy, plain reversal, a wrong-variant rule (the informative floor beside copy), a random legal output, the majority answer for \task{T3} and the mean human.

\paragraph{Tokenizer profile.}
For every tokenizer we measure, over the standard syllable inventory in running-text position, tokens per syllable, the share of single-token syllables, whether a syllable is split, and, among split syllables, \emph{boundary alignment}: the share of token boundaries strictly inside the syllable that fall on an onset$|$rime, glide$|$nucleus or nucleus$|$coda boundary, adapting STAD \citep{2026-acl-long-634} to sub-syllabic boundaries; a boundary between a base letter and its combining mark is never aligned. Under NFD we also record whether the tone mark is isolated in a token of its own. For the Gemma~3 tokenizer these are 1.78 tokens per syllable, 29.5\% single-token, alignment 0.94 and an onset$|$rime split in 51.5\% of syllables under NFC, against 2.83, 7.1\%, 0.27 and a tone mark isolated in 41.6\% of syllables under NFD; the identity normalizer leaves 96.6\% of NFD words with different ids, so it passes through. \placeholder{Table of all panel tokenizers with the census verdict in Appendix~\ref{app:compute}.}

\paragraph{Statistics.}
The base pair is the sampled unit and the only cluster: every item derived from it, every paraphrase and every model's answers to it form one cluster, and paraphrases are aggregated to the item level. Every interval is a bootstrap over base pairs (2,000 replicates, stratified by pair type, BCa where a cell has at least 50 pairs) \citep{2411.00640}, and every comparison is paired by item inside each replicate; McNemar's exact mid-$p$ test \citep{mcnemar-1947-note,P18-1128} is applied to base-pair-aggregated outcomes. Holm's step-down correction \citep{holm-1979-simple,Q17-1033} is applied within declared families: for each model, the six intervention effects (three arms on \noilai{} and on XCOPA); for \hyp{4} and \hyp{6}, the per-model tests across the panel; Table~\ref{tab:main} is estimation only. A claim about models in general rests on a pooled random-effects estimate over the per-model paired effects, never on a count of significant cells. For what predicts failure (\hyp{1}), item correctness is regressed on tokenization covariates with a within--between decomposition: whether the syllable is split, tokens per syllable centred within model, and alignment among split syllables (alignment is undefined for a single token and collinear with count otherwise), with lexicality, corpus log frequency of each syllable, variant and task; the primary fit is a logistic regression with model fixed effects and model $\times$ fragmentation interactions and two-way (base pair, model) cluster-robust standard errors, the generalization check a random-slope model, and a GEE clustered by base pair the robustness fit. For the re-encoding arms the headline is the within-item average treatment effect per model; the descriptive decompositions are in Section~\ref{sec:counterfactuals}. At 1,000 independent items and 70\% accuracy the half-width of a 95\% interval is 2.8 points, and under the design effect of base-pair clustering 3.4--5 points; a paired 5-point difference is detected with 94\% power only when few items are discordant, and with about 65\% when errors are independent; 350 items per cell resolve a 10-point contrast at 80\% power, and the 125-item API cells resolve 11--17 points, so cell-level claims rest on the open models. The pilot measured the intraclass correlation and the discordance rates that fixed these sizes before any test item was run. Only 16\% of 445 reviewed LLM benchmarks reported uncertainty or tests \citep{2511.04703}; every table here does.
