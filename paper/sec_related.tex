\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Tokenization and sub-word tasks.}
Subword vocabularies \citep{P16-1162,D18-2012} fragment some languages far more than others, at a cost in compute and accuracy \citep{2021-acl-long-243,2023-emnlp-main-614,2023-findings-acl-350}, and compression alone does not predict what a tokenizer does to a model \citep{2023-acl-long-284,2024-emnlp-main-40,2024-findings-naacl-247}. Subword embeddings encode character identity \citep{2022-naacl-main-179,2022-naacl-main-373}, character-level competence emerges late and depends on merge rules \citep{2025-emnlp-main-1434,2026-eacl-long-282}, and models reconstruct characters in intermediate layers \citep{2025-findings-emnlp-719}. CUTE, EXECUTE and SubTokenTest test spelling, counting and manipulation \citep{2024-emnlp-main-177,2025-findings-acl-95,2026-acl-long-915} without Vietnamese; PACUTE extends the design to Filipino syllabification and morphology \citep{pacute-2026-filipino}. Tokenization's causal effect has been estimated by training controlled model families \citep{2025-acl-long-1374,toksuite-2025} and by re-segmenting fixed models: alternative segmentations of the same code points lower accuracy across 27 languages (\placeholder{relative drops of 10--24\%; page-level check of arXiv 2607.26831 pending, Vietnamese coverage unconfirmed}) \citep{ghosh-jyothi-2026-noncanonical}, inconsistent segmentations hurt Japanese grammaticality \citep{2025-acl-short-75}, and spelling variants tokenize differently \citep{2025-findings-acl-572}. Diacritics are the usual casualty of preprocessing \citep{2025-naacl-short-25}, and Arabic diacritization fragments tokens and lowers scores \citep{2026-findings-eacl-22}. Sub-syllabic tokenizers exist for Korean jamo \citep{2020-lrec-1-429}, for phonemes \citep{2026-acl-long-1872} and, for Vietnamese and Mandarin, by factorizing syllables into onset, rime and tone before pretraining \citep{2609.21362}; a Southeast Asian tokenizer comparison trains new models \citep{2606.15044}. These works change the tokenizer; we diagnose deployed ones with the code points users type.

\paragraph{Vietnamese NLP.}
Vietnamese resources are word- or sentence-level: toolkits and pretrained models \citep{N18-5012,2020-findings-emnlp-92,2022-naacl-srw-18,2311.02945}, knowledge and understanding suites \citep{2025-acl-long-563,2024-findings-naacl-261}, diacritic removal as one perturbation in a broad evaluation \citep{2024-findings-naacl-182}, dialect datasets \citep{2024-emnlp-main-426,2608.10414}, monosyllabic morphology injected into encoders \citep{2026-acl-long-472} and tone probed in speech models \citep{2024-naacl-long-239}. The only computational treatment of nói lái detects attested examples in classical text with rules and an $n$-gram model \citep{Y18-1063}; no study generates, decodes or judges it, and none asks an LLM to.

\paragraph{Wordplay and phonology in LLMs.}
Puns are the wordplay LLMs are usually tested on \citep{S17-2005,2024-emnlp-main-657,2025-emnlp-main-1419}; a Russian headline corpus lists spoonerism among its wordplay types \citep{2025-ranlp-1-15}. Phonological benchmarks test grapheme-to-phoneme conversion, syllable counting and rhyme in English \citep{2024-knowllm-1-1} and homophony, rhyme and tone in Chinese, where models recall pronunciations but cannot use them \citep{2026-acl-long-1041}; \citet{2026-acl-long-634} relate tokenization to phonological knowledge through an alignment metric over English syllabification, which our boundary alignment adapts. In Chinese and Filipino the phonology is latent behind the script; Vietnamese writes onset, rime and tone in the string, so a failure to manipulate them is attributable to access, not knowledge.

\paragraph{Benchmark construction and pre-registration.}
Our design follows the contrastive logic of minimal-pair suites \citep{2020-tacl-1-25,P19-1116} and the reporting standards that benchmark critiques ask for and few benchmarks meet: construct validity \citep{2511.04703,2021-naacl-main-385}, power and error bars \citep{2020-emnlp-main-745,2411.00640,P18-1128}, contamination control \citep{2023-emnlp-main-308,2022-acl-short-18,2023-findings-emnlp-722} and data statements \citep{Q18-1041}; only \placeholder{16\%; page-level check of arXiv 2511.04703 pending} of 445 reviewed LLM benchmarks reported uncertainty or tests \citep{2511.04703}, and every table here does. Pre-registration has been proposed for NLP \citep{2021-naacl-main-51} and practised rarely \citep{2024-lrec-main-809}; ours is two-stage and dated by commit.

\paragraph{Probing and intervention.}
Probes show that information is present, not that it is used \citep{2022-cl-1-7,2021-eacl-main-295}; control tasks \citep{D19-1275} and structural baselines guard against trivially decodable targets. Activation patching locates where information is used \citep{meng-etal-2022-locating,heimersheim-nanda-2024-use,2021-acl-long-144,2024-acl-long-785}, and interchange interventions have induced character-level structure in subword models \citep{2023-findings-acl-770}. The gap, stated exactly: this is, to our knowledge, the first LLM study of nói lái and the first generative, decoding and validity benchmark of it; no study treats spoonerism as a controlled transformation task; and none compares canonically equivalent encodings, or tone-placement conventions, of a tonal Latin script on deployed models.
