\paragraph{Pre-registered hypotheses.}
The predictions below were fixed in a dated commit before any model was run (Appendix~\ref{app:prereg}). \hyp{1} (\emph{alignment over count}): in the regression of Section~\ref{sec:setup}, the within-model coefficient of boundary alignment is positive, that of tokens per syllable is negative, and the standardized alignment coefficient is the larger in absolute value; the cluster-bootstrap interval of the standardized difference excludes zero in the fixed-effect specification and in the random-slope check. \hyp{2} (\emph{a whole-syllable vocabulary}): PhoGPT-4B-Chat, whose 20,480-piece vocabulary stores most syllables as single tokens, has a lower per-syllable negative log-likelihood on a held-out Vietnamese text sample than Qwen3.5-4B and Gemma~3 4B \emph{and} a lower \task{T1} accuracy on the same items; this is a descriptive dissociation on one model, not a population claim. \hyp{6} (\emph{memorization}): exact attested items outscore generated items matched on variant, task, output lexicality, spelling trigger and tone class, pooled over models; and the guided-instruction contamination rate is positive on attested items and near zero on the matched generated ones. The memorization inference rests on the guided-instruction test \citep{2308.08493}, closed-book recall of well-known nói lái, the conditional log-probability contrast and the name-only prompt, not on the accuracy gap alone, which conflates salience with memorization.
