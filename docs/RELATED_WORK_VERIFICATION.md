# Related-work verification (30 September 2026)

From the bibliographer lens. **Method.** WebFetch is egress-blocked for arxiv.org and aclanthology.org (`EGRESS_BLOCKED`). WebSearch covered 44 identifiers before the session's 200-call budget ran out. Every ACL Anthology ID was therefore checked against the Anthology's own metadata (`acl-org/acl-anthology`, `data/xml/`, fetched from raw.githubusercontent.com): title, authors, volume, pages, DOI and award fields below come from that XML. arXiv IDs not covered by search were confirmed through GitHub-hosted arXiv RSS mirrors of cs.CL or the papers' official repositories. **"Verified" means the identifier resolves to a paper whose title matches the plan's description; it does not mean the numbers the plan attributes to the paper were checked** (those are listed in section 2). `paper/references.bib` holds the BibTeX for the verified entries only; the novelty sweep's closest-work items (section 4) are NOT in the bib until they are verified by hand.

Totals: 54 Anthology IDs (50 papers + 4 front-matter volumes) all resolve; 15 arXiv IDs all exist; 1 PMLR entry exists; 2 Benjamins catalog links: one title confirmed, one unverifiable. No fabricated identifier was found.

## 2. Mismatches and suspicious entries

1. **Companion files were missing.** The plan says the 190-URL source list lives in this file and in `paper/references.bib`. Neither existed before this session; this file and the bib now seed them. The plan's "about 190 URLs" include many non-paper sources (ARR pages, blogs, vendor docs) that are not bibliography entries and are not verified here.
2. **2026.socon-1.7 contradicts a Bundle B novelty claim.** The plan: "apart from a layer-wise probe of politeness in a Japanese BERT (2026.socon-1.7), we found no interpretability study … and none that tests such features causally." The title is "… A Layerwise Probing **and CLS Activation Patching** Study": activation patching is a causal intervention. Soften to "none in a generative LLM / none for reciprocal address" after reading the paper.
3. **Preprints with archival versions (cite the archival version):** arXiv 2504.02768 → **2026.tacl-1.10** (TACL 14, pp. 193–216, DOI 10.1162/tacl.a.600); arXiv 2211.02570 → **2022.emnlp-main.731**; arXiv 1709.09500 → **Q17-1033** (TACL 5, pp. 471–486); 2308.08493 and 2310.11324 are ICLR 2024 papers; 2511.04703 appeared at NeurIPS 2025 Datasets & Benchmarks per snippet [UNCERTAIN: exact citation].
