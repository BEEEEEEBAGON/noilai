"Early signal" is the observable that says the risk is materializing; "owner" is who acts (author = only the author can; implementer = the next code session; validators = the native validators; co-author = the ARR-qualified collaborator once recruited). Binding mitigations are cross-referenced to `docs/DESIGN_DECISIONS.md` (DD §). **Revised 30 September 2026 after the red-team review**: rows marked (RT n) were added or changed for red-team objection n, logged in DD §14.
