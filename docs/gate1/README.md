# Gate 1 packet: index

Gate 1 (18 October 2026) is native validation: 2–3 adult native-speaker validators check the generator's output, the
attested rows and the spelling conventions, after a calibration round. The human baseline uses the same packet's
recruitment and consent and runs after Gate 1. This page lists every document in the packet, who reads it, and the
timeline. Status: draft, 1 October 2026; nothing has been sent and no judgment exists.

Readers: **author** = runs the step, never sent; **validators** = sent to the 2–3 validators; **respondents** = sent to
the human-baseline respondents (different people from the validators).

## Documents

| document | what it is | reader |
|---|---|---|
| `docs/gate1/VALIDATION_PROTOCOL.md` | the validation design: Parts A–E and their sizes, the Part B probability sample and planted controls, the timeline, agreement (Krippendorff's α, AC1), the adjudication rule, the H6 attested-floor arithmetic | author |
| `docs/gate1/RECRUITMENT.md` | the two invitation messages (validators; baseline respondents) in Vietnamese and English, how to send them, answers to likely questions | author sends; validators and respondents receive their message |
| `docs/CONSENT_FORM.md` | bilingual consent for both roles: purpose, time, unpaid, withdrawal until the results freeze, data kept, no ethics review | validators and respondents (as a PDF) |
| `docs/VALIDATOR_INSTRUCTIONS.md` | the guidelines in Vietnamese and English: syllable structure and the six kinds, worked examples, the calibration round, every sheet column, `RULE?` comments, how disagreements are resolved | validators (all but its last section, "Notes for the author") |
| `data/validation/validation_<V>.xlsx` | one workbook per validator (sheets `A_calibration`, `B_items`, `C_attested`, `D1_production`, `D2_qu`, `D3_conventions`, `E_t2gold`), built by `make validation`, imported into Google Sheets; git-ignored | validators (their own workbook only) |
| `A_calibration_key.json`, `B_key.json`, `D_engine.json`, `E_key.json`, `validation_manifest.json` (in `data/validation/`) | the author's keys and the packet manifest; never sent | author |
| `scripts/make_validation_forms.py`, `noilai/validation.py`, `noilai/stats/agreement.py` | builds the packet (`make validation`), scores the calibration and the returns, computes α and AC1 and applies the adjudication rule (`make validation-score`), writes the blind third-validator sheet (`adjudication-sheet`) | author |
| `data/attested_candidates.tsv` | web-sourced attested candidates with source URL, collection, evidence and licence note; judged in Part C | author (validators see the rows, not the file) |
| `docs/gate1/HUMAN_BASELINE_PROTOCOL.md` | the run-ready human-baseline protocol: who, items, same format as the models, runbook with commands, exclusions, analysis, data handling, timeline | author |
| `docs/HUMAN_BASELINE_FORM.md` | the baseline design (6 anchors + 240 double-judged items, cyclic double coverage) and the form layout | author |
| one Google Form per respondent | built by `data/human/build_forms.gs` (generated by `google-form`) in the author's own Google account | respondents (one link each) |
| `docs/CHECKLIST_DRAFT.md` | draft answers to the ARR Responsible NLP checklist; B4 and D1–D5 follow this packet | author |
| `docs/DEVIATIONS.md` | the log of design changes with reasons (DD 10.1 and 10.2 amendments) | author |

## What only the author can do

Recruiting, sending, building the packet from the private release files, running the Apps Script in the author's own
Google account, and downloading responses: each such step, with its commands and expected time, belongs in
**`BLOCKED.md`** at the repository root (the baseline steps are listed in `docs/gate1/HUMAN_BASELINE_PROTOCOL.md` §10
until they are added there).
